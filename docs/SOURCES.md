# Company prep sources

Every company page in `content/companies/` is paraphrased from the pages below. We never copy interview questions or problem statements; practice sets use DevPREP's own problems. A stage is marked **Official** only when a company page states it. Anything else (round counts, focus areas, how candidates describe the loop) is marked **Reported**, and the focus areas are an editorial estimate, labelled as such in the app.

Each page's `asOf` is `2026-10`. Hiring processes change often and vary by role, level and location. When updating a company, re-check every link, update `asOf` and the access dates here, and keep paraphrasing.

## Google

- [How we hire](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/how-we-hire/) (official, accessed 2026-10-26): application, recruiter contact, assessments, interviews and the decision.
- [Interview tips](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/interview-tips/) (official, accessed 2026-10-26): technical and behavioural interview advice, and how candidates are assessed. Also the behavioural section's link.
- [Ten things we know to be true](https://about.google/company-info/philosophy/) (official, accessed 2026-10-26): the company philosophy, summarised under values.
